# topic: What is generative AI content like ChatGPT as an economic condition?
# model: 27B
# provisional: 0

Should we pay for the prompt or the output?
Letting a machine do your job: is that laziness or smart business?
Once AI writes it all, what happens to our salaries and rent money?
Paying someone to type words into a bot feels like paying for nothing.

The writer who used AI to finish the job in minutes gets paid less than the one who took three days.
Is that fair, or is it just how money works now? If your boss can replace you with a prompt, what do
actually own anymore: your skills, or only the time you sell them for? When everything costs nothing
to make, does anything have value at all?

Look at your paycheck. How much of it is paid for by a machine that costs nothing to copy?
Every time you ask this tool to write, do you feel like an owner or just another worker in the loop?
Everyone can be a creator now. Does that make art more valuable because there's so much of it, or is
because no one has to pay for skill anymore?
