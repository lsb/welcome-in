# topic: What is generative AI content like ChatGPT as a psychological condition?
# model: 27B
# provisional: 0

Should we treat a chatbot like a friend or a mirror?
Losing your train of thought to an algorithm: is that laziness or evolution?
Or are you just outsourcing the hard work of being human.
Prompting for compliments instead of earning them from people who know you, what does it cost us in?

The machine never gets tired, so why do you?
Is it comforting to be understood by something that doesn't care about your feelings?
Are we talking to a tool or confessing to a mirror?
To whom are you really speaking when the screen replies instantly and perfectly?

Look at your phone. When you type a prompt instead of writing the email yourself, do you feel like a
editor or an author? If ChatGPT finishes your sentence before you think it, are you being helped or
emptied out? Does getting exactly what you asked for make you trust the answer more than if someone
began to disagree with you?
